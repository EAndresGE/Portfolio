Remote Sensing & Fire Impact Analysis — Australia

The challenge

South-west Australia experiences severe fire seasons that fundamentally alter landscape ecology. The challenge was to use remote sensing data to quantify and visualise landscape conditions before and after a major bushfire — demonstrating how satellite data can support post-fire management decisions.

The approach

NetCDF data from the NCI data portal was analysed in MATLAB. Sub-sampling scripts extracted tree cover, soil exposure, and soil moisture layers for the area of interest. Data manipulation in MATLAB produced before/after visualisations of fire impact, with code structured for easy reuse on future datasets.

Key outcomes

Pre- and post-fire landscape conditions quantified using remote sensing indices

NetCDF satellite data ingested, processed, and visualised entirely in MATLAB

Reusable analysis scripts produced for tree cover, soil exposure, and moisture mapping
