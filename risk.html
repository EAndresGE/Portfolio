Avalanche Risk Modelling — Valais, Switzerland

The challenge

The authorities of the Valais canton needed a GIS-based analysis to prioritise towns and villages for avalanche risk mitigation measures. The challenge was to translate multiple physical and spatial risk factors into a single ranked risk surface that could directly inform resource allocation decisions.

The approach

Multiple risk factor layers were prepared and combined using weighted overlay analysis in ArcGIS Pro — including slope angle, aspect, elevation, proximity to known avalanche paths, and population exposure. Each factor was reclassified and weighted by relative importance to produce a composite risk index. Final outputs ranked settlements by risk exposure.

Key outcomes

Composite avalanche risk index produced for the Valais canton

Settlements ranked by risk exposure to prioritise mitigation investment

Multi-factor weighted overlay combining terrain, path proximity, and population data
