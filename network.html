Light Rail Accessibility — Network Analysis, Canberra

The challenge

Evaluate whether the proposed Stage 1 ACT light rail system in Canberra would effectively serve the population. How accessible are the proposed stops by foot and by road, and does the catchment area justify the investment?

The approach

Network analysis was conducted using ArcGIS Network Analyst and QGIS. Service area polygons were generated for each proposed stop at multiple walking and driving time thresholds. Population data was intersected with catchment areas to quantify residents within practical access distances, and results were compared across all stops.

Key outcomes

Walking and driving catchment areas calculated for every proposed stop

Population within each catchment quantified using census data intersection

Stop-by-stop accessibility comparison produced to support planning recommendations
